Weer gives you all the features you need for shortening and managing links for free, and gives you the ability to share links with anyone in the fastest possible way. Based on our measures, if you are talking to someone and want to share a link with them, with Weer you can do it in less than a few seconds, much faster than any other URL shortener out there.

No sign-up required

Paste a URL and get a short link instantly, no account, no clutter, no popups, no extra junk. Want to keep things organized? Create a free account anytime to manage your links and claim a clean, custom username.

Secure by design

Not only is every submitted URL checked by Google Web Risk for safety, but if a link cannot be 100% trusted, we will also show you exactly where you're headed before you're redirected instead of an automatic redirect.

Completely free

All features of the app are free with no hidden fees or subscriptions, and no ads. No premium features locked away.

Free QR codes

Every shortened link comes with a free, permanent QR code that you can download and keep.

Share a link in seconds

Choose an Ultra or Digit code and share a link that's as easy to say out loud as it is to type. Tell someone "go to weer.pro/198" and they're there instantly.

Track visits

See exactly how your links are performing. Total clicks and unique visitors are tracked automatically for every link you create.
